## Background

The website StackSats.how was an excellent curated resource for earning small amounts of Bitcoin through legitimate websites and services. Unlike many similar sites, it was not simply a collection of referral links. It focused on genuinely useful services such as games, educational platforms, cashback, browser rewards, and Lightning-enabled applications.

Since StackSats disappeared, there does not appear to be a comparable resource.

## Project Goal

Create a modern successor to StackSats that focuses on **quality rather than quantity**.

Rather than listing every site that offers Bitcoin rewards, the aim is to recommend only services that have been personally tested or have a strong reputation within the Bitcoin community.

## Possible Structure

Categories could include:

* Bitcoin faucets
* Educational sites
* Games that pay Lightning rewards
* Cashback services
* Browsers and search engines
* Shopping rewards
* Writing and content creation
* Mobile apps
* Community reward platforms
* Promotions and temporary campaigns

## Principles

* Only list legitimate sites.
* Avoid scams, cloud mining schemes and "deposit first" offers.
* Referral links should be optional and clearly identified.
* Honest reviews instead of promotional descriptions.
* Explain exactly how each site works.
* Include realistic earning expectations (e.g. sats per day or per hour).
* Include withdrawal limits and Lightning support.
* Mark services that have stopped paying or become inactive rather than deleting them.

## Extra Features

Each listing could include:

* Overall rating
* Time required
* Difficulty
* Typical earnings
* Withdrawal method
* Minimum withdrawal
* Country restrictions
* Last tested date
* Community comments or reports

## Relationship with FaucetList

This could either become a new section of FaucetList or, preferably, a separate website with FaucetList linking to it.

The distinction would be:

* FaucetList = Bitcoin faucets only.
* New site = Every legitimate way to stack sats.

This keeps both projects focused while allowing them to complement each other.

## Long-term Vision

Become the most trusted directory of legitimate Bitcoin earning opportunities.

The emphasis should always be on helping users spend their time wisely, rather than maximising referral income. If a site is poor value, say so. If something pays well, explain why. The aim is to save people from wasting hours on low-quality offers and instead guide them towards the genuinely worthwhile ways of stacking sats.

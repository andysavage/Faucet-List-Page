About Faucet List

Keep track of all the sites you visit regularly with personal timers and optional notifications.

🕐 Timer Management

  • Add sites with a name, URL, and timer duration (in minutes)
  • Visual countdown progress bars show time remaining
  • List auto-sorts: ready sites appear at the top, then by time remaining
  • Click Claim to open the site and reset its timer
  • Edit or delete sites anytime

💾 Data Storage

  • Guest mode: Your list is stored in your browser – no account needed
  • Signed in: Your list syncs to the cloud so you can access it anywhere
  • Works offline using your browser's local storage
  • Local data automatically uploads when you first sign in

🔔 Browser Notifications

  • Toggle notifications on/off with the bell button in the header
  • Get notified when any timer reaches zero
  • Works even when the tab is in the background or browser is minimized
  • Requires the page to be open (notifications stop if you close the tab)
  • Your preference is saved – notifications are off by default

📱 Responsive Design

  • Adapts to any screen size – desktop, tablet, or mobile
  • Compact display on smaller screens
